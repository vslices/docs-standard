# Resolved research pins

This directory preserves research pins whose primary uncertainty has been resolved.

A resolved pin is not discarded history.

It should preserve the reconstructible path from hypothesis to resolution:

~~~text
hypothesis
-> accumulated evidence
-> tensions or counterexamples
-> resolution
-> normative consequence
~~~

A pin may be resolved because its hypothesis was:

- promoted into normative Docs Standard semantics;
- absorbed into another concept or responsibility;
- rejected by evidence;
- reframed into a more precise research question.

Moving a pin here should happen only after its central research question no longer remains open in its original form.

Do not rewrite resolved pins to make the final result appear inevitable.
