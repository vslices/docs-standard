# Closed and open question complementarity

## Research question

> Can closed questions and open questions complement each other inside Docs Standard instead of treating closed questions as categorically inferior?

## Observation

Domain Vocabulary exposed a useful counterexample to the heuristic that closed questions should always be replaced by open questions.

Historical closed questions in term-origin:

~~~text
¿Es lenguaje observado en el dominio?
¿Es lenguaje técnico?
¿Fue introducido deliberadamente?
¿Proviene de otra disciplina o sistema?
¿Su significado fue adaptado?
~~~

Promoted open questions:

~~~text
¿En qué lenguaje, disciplina o sistema se origina?
¿Cómo llegó a usarse en este contexto?
¿Qué significado tenía en su origen?
¿Qué significado fue adaptado al incorporarlo aquí?
¿Qué parte fue introducida deliberadamente?
~~~

The two forms can complement each other.

~~~text
closed question
    -> classifies a known dimension

open question
    -> reveals the concrete form of the phenomenon
~~~

The problem is not that a question is closed. The problem is when the closed answer ends the inquiry while important semantics remain undiscovered.

## Form-like authoring hypothesis

A future materialization may combine structured facets with open explanation.

Example:

~~~text
[ ] observed in domain
[ ] technical
[ ] deliberately introduced
[ ] imported from another discipline
[ ] adapted meaning

Then:
Where did it originate?
How did it enter this context?
What meaning changed?
~~~

This is a materialization possibility, not normative semantics yet.

## Open questions

- When does a closed question add useful classification rather than redundant confirmation?
- Should closed questions require an open refinement?
- Can enum-like answers replace several booleans?
- Can closed classifications be inferred from open answers?
- Should Tooling support structured facets plus explanatory questions?
- How should AI authoring use classifications without stopping exploration too early?
